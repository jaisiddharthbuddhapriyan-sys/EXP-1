 # EXP-1: VERIFICATION OF KIRCHHOFF'S LAWS

## AIM

a. To verify Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL) for the given circuit.

b. To verify Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) for the given circuits.

## THEORY

**KVL (Kirchhoff's Voltage Law):** Kirchhoff's Voltage Law states that the algebraic sum of all voltages around any closed loop in a circuit is zero.

Formula: V = V1 + V2 + V3

**KCL (Kirchhoff's Current Law):** Kirchhoff's Current Law states that the total current entering a junction is equal to the total current leaving the junction.

Formula: I(in) = I1 + I2 + I3

## PROCEDURE

### A. KVL

1. Connect the circuit as shown in the KVL circuit diagram.
2. Set the regulated power supply voltage to zero.
3. Check the voltmeters for proper zero readings.
4. Switch on the power supply and set the input voltage to 10 V.
5. Measure the voltage drop across each resistor.
6. Record the readings and calculate the total voltage drop.
7. Verify that the total voltage drop is approximately equal to the supply voltage.

### B. KCL

1. Connect the circuit as shown in the KCL circuit diagram.
2. Set the regulated power supply voltage to zero.
3. Check the ammeters for proper zero readings.
4. Switch on the power supply and set the input voltage to 10 V.
5. Measure the incoming current and the currents flowing through the branches.
6. Record the readings and calculate the total outgoing current.
7. Verify that the incoming current equals the total outgoing current.

### A. Verification of KVL

Supply voltage = 10 V

Voltage across R1 = 1.42 V

Voltage across R2 = 5.34 V

Voltage across R3 = 3.20 V

According to KVL:

Total voltage drop = V1 + V2 + V3

= 1.42 + 5.34 + 3.20

= 9.96 V

Supply voltage = 10 V

Total voltage drop = 9.96 V

Therefore, 10 V ≈ 9.96 V.

Hence, KVL is verified.

### B. Verification of KCL

Incoming current = 0.50 A

Current through R2 = 0.25 A

Current through R3 = 0.16 A

Current through R4 = 0.09 A

According to KCL:

Incoming current = Total outgoing current

= 0.25 + 0.16 + 0.09

= 0.50 A

Therefore, incoming current = outgoing current = 0.50 A.

Hence, KCL is verified.
